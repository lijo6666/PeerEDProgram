About the system

What is the Peer Education System?

The Peer Education System (PES) of CHRIST (Deemed to be University) is a dynamic student development initiative built on the principles of mutual understanding, support, trust, leadership, and community engagement.

The programme was conceptualized and developed by the Centre for Counselling & Health Services (CCHS) and was officially launched at the Central Campus in 2009. It provides an avenue where students support fellow students.

PES serves as a platform where students are trained to become Peer Educators who contribute to building a supportive, inclusive, and emotionally healthy campus environment.

Peer Influence

Why Peer Education Matters

Young adults spend a significant portion of their university lives interacting with peers. These relationships profoundly shape behavior, values, and emotional adaptability.

Peers Shape Experiences

Peer relationships directly influence everyday student experiences and long-term habits:

  • Emotional well-being & adaptiveness
  • Confidence and self-identity
  • Decision-making in crucial situations
  • Communication and social development
  • Academic adjustment and grades
  • Unlocking leadership potentials

Healthy Relationships Foster

When peer environments are positive, supportive, and active, students thrive in multiple domains:

  • Strong, authentic friendships and trust
  • Mutual empathy and active support
  • Accelerated personal growth and maturity
  • Positive, ethical behavior patterns
  • A genuine sense of community belonging

* Note: Unmanaged peer environments can also trigger stress and conflict. PES acts as a positive intervention to guide peer cultures healthily.

Process Path

How PES Works

A step-by-step interactive journey representing the onboarding, training, and operational framework of Peer Educators.

Step 1

Recruitment

Students are formally invited to submit applications to join the Peer Educator system.

Step 2

Selection

Candidates are evaluated based on leadership traits, service mindset, and reliability.

Step 3

Training

Selected candidates undergo structured modules: communication, active listening, and teamwork.

Step 4

PES Activities

Students engage with peers on campus through structured developmental initiatives.

Step 5

Awareness

Peer Educators conduct focused classrooms sessions and campus stalls on emotional health.

Step 6

Student Support

Creating approachable networks and safe discussion circles for peer wellness.

Step 7

Leadership

Expanding leadership capacities by coordinating teams, publications, and events.

Step 8

Recognition

Appreciation letters and certificates awarded during the final valedictory ceremony.

Role of Peer Educators

Connectors

Leaders
